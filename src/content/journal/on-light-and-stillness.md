There is a moment, just after the sun drops below a ridge, when the world stops
performing. The harsh contrast is gone. Shadows soften. Colour drains toward a
single cool note. This is the light I wait for.

## Patience over capture

Most of the work is not pressing the shutter. It is standing still long enough
for the scene to settle — and for me to settle with it. A photograph made in a
hurry almost always looks hurried.

## Restraint as a discipline

The temptation is to add: more contrast, more saturation, more drama. The
discipline is to remove, until only the necessary remains. An image that holds
its breath asks the viewer to hold theirs.
