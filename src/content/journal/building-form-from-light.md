title: "Building form from light"
description: "Carrying a photographer's sensibility into three dimensions."
date: 2024-12-03

---

When I started making 3D work, I tried to light it the way I light a portrait:
one soft key, a low fill, and a great deal of empty space. The render engine did
not care that I had spent years with a camera — but the instinct carried over.

## One object, carefully lit

A single sculptural form, turning slowly in an empty room, can say more than a
crowded scene. The constraint forces every decision to matter: the material, the
angle of the key, the depth of the shadow.

## The same eye, a new medium

Photography taught me to see. 3D simply gave me a new place to look.
